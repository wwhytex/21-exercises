Pseudocode Exercises — English

🟢 Level 1 — Input, output and variables

1. Hello, student!
Write an algorithm that asks for the user's name and displays:

Hello, [name]! Welcome to programming.

2. Age
Ask for a person's name and age and display a message with that data.

3. Sum of two numbers
Read two numbers and display their sum.

4. Mathematical operations
Read two numbers and display:
- Sum
- Difference
- Product
- Quotient

5. Area of a rectangle
Ask for the length and width of a rectangle and calculate its area.

6. Average of three grades
Read a student's three grades and calculate the average.

🟡 Level 2 — If conditions

7. Positive or negative number
Read a number and indicate whether it is:
- Positive
- Negative
- Zero

8. Even or odd
Read an integer and indicate whether it is even or odd.

9. Larger of two numbers
Read two numbers and indicate which is larger.
If they are equal, it should indicate:

The numbers are equal.

10. Student pass/fail
Read a grade between 0 and 20.
If the grade is 10 or higher, display:

Pass

Otherwise:

Fail

11. Largest of three numbers
Read three numbers and determine which is the largest.

🟠 Level 3 — Everyday problems

12. Simple calculator
Ask for two numbers and an operation (+, -, * or /).
The algorithm should perform the chosen operation.

13. Discounted price
Ask for the price of a product.
If the price is above €100, apply a 10% discount.
Display the final price.

14. Temperature
Ask for a temperature in degrees Celsius and display:
- "Very cold" if below 10 ºC
- "Pleasant" between 10 ºC and 25 ºC
- "Hot" above 25 ºC

15. Legal age
Ask for a person's age and indicate whether they are:
- A minor
- An adult

🔵 Level 4 — While loops

16. Count from 1 to 10
Use While to display the numbers from 1 to 10.

17. Countdown
Use While to display the numbers from 10 down to 1.

18. Even numbers
Display all even numbers between 1 and 20 using While.

19. Sum of numbers
Ask the user for 5 numbers and calculate their sum.

20. Average of several numbers
Ask for 5 grades and calculate the average.

🔴 Final challenge

21. Guessing game

The computer has a secret number between 1 and 100.
The user must try to guess the number.
A cada tentativa... — wait, on each attempt, the program should indicate:
- "The number is higher"
- "The number is lower"
- "Congratulations! You got it!"

The program ends when the user guesses correctly.
